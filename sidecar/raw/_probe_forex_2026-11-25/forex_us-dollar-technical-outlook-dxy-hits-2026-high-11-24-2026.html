US Dollar Technical Outlook: DXY Multi-Timeframe Analysis

The U.S. dollar index is flashing exhaustion signals after a five-week rally stalled just short of a major resistance confluence. Michael Boutros, StoneX Media Senior Market Analyst, walks through the weekly, daily and four-hour DXY charts and the support and resistance zones framing the yearly uptrend. A daily outside day reversal within the uptrend was followed by daily RSI momentum slipping back below 70. Core PCE, the Federal Reserve's preferred inflation gauge, arrives alongside personal spending, a Q3 GDP update and the Beige Book. Thinner liquidity in the Thanksgiving holiday-shortened week can exaggerate currency moves late in the week. Fed rate expectations heading into 2027 keep a bid under the dollar through the interest rate differential.
