### GOLD — CORE TRADING PRINCIPLES (extracted and categorized):

#### A. ENTRY TIMING & RETEST RULES (CRITICAL — NOT IN v5.1)

- **1-2-3 Retest Law**: "There will always be AT LEAST 2 retests before a move fully pushes through. Do not enter on the first retest."
  - 1-2 = scalping opportunity
  - 3+ = swing trading entry
  - "DRAW THE CHART each retest point and number them"
  
- **"Wait for the bounce" rule**: "ALWAYS wait for the 'bounce' before entering to know EXACTLY WHERE the price is going to move from. Identify the pressure zone that will drive the move BY LETTING THE MARKET SHOW YOU."

- **No virgin zone entries (unless BoM)**: "NO entries on virgin zones unless they are beginning of the move. Make this a rule."

- **SL timing**: "After the 3 has occurred and the FULL MOVE STARTS, THEN liquidity zone reinforcements are valid and will most likely NOT get stopped out."

#### B. BoM vs MoM ZONE HIERARCHY (DEEPLY DETAILED — PARTIALLY IN v5.1)

- **BoM = 100% guaranteed reaction**: "Unmitigated Beginning/Source of move zones are 100% GUARANTEED to have a reaction when touched."
- **MoM = likely to be pierced**: "'Middle of Move' zones tend to get pierced the most often. Especially if there is strong liquidity from the opposing side."
- **MoM zones are for OPPOSING LIQUIDITY TP targets, NOT entries**: "You should mostly IGNORE MoM zones for entries. ONLY take entries on BoM zones. 90% more likely to catch a winning trade."
- **MoM exception**: Only valid when (a) aligned with trend + already tested with strong reaction, (b) aligned with key S/R level, (c) the opposing liquidity reaction is weak
- **BoM priority rule**: "AVOID 'Middle of Move' zones unless they've ALREADY been retested with strong reaction at least once. ALWAYS enter on BEGINNING OF THE MOVE zones."

#### C. 4H ZONE MECHANICS (DETAILED — NOT IN v5.1)

- **4H MoM zones are "one and done"**: "If they are 100% unmitigated there WILL be a reaction on the first retest, but often it is a minor reaction followed by a strong pierce."
- **4H BoM reaction cascade**: "BoM Unmitigated 4H liquidity will usually pierce through everything until it reacts to liquidity of the same element."
- **Each timeframe's liquidity is its own element**: "4H liquidity → 4H liquidity. 1H liquidity → 1H liquidity. 30m → 30m. LTF liquidity more likely to be absorbed by HTF liquidity — but usually never more than 2 dimensions up."
- **1H zones are king**: "1H zones are still king."

#### D. ZONE REACTION COUNT (NEW — NOT IN v5.1)

- **Three-reaction rule**: "Trend Aligned Zones that cause a strong reaction will almost always have THREE strong reactions before the zone fails and gets pierced."
- **Reaction counting**: "If there is such a zone between current PA and BoM zone, assume price will fully liquidate this initial pressure zone before touching the BoM zone."
- **Zone juice check**: "If this pressure zone still has juice left (< 3 strong reactions, 3rd incredibly powerful implies 4th incoming) — use this pressure zone for entries/TPs. Leave BoM zone for later."

#### E. SEQUENTIAL LIQUIDITY SWEEP (NEW — NOT IN v5.1)

- **"The price action will ALWAYS sweep liquidity zone by zone in a sequential order. It will NEVER skip a liquidity zone."**
- **"Always look to see which liquidity zones have been retested & which ones have not. The zones that have NOT been retested yet WILL be retested. This is your entry point."**
- **This is a CRITICAL principle for trade planning — v5.1 doesn't account for unmitigated liquidity zone sequencing.**

   - BoM zones = ENTRY candidate (100% reaction guarantee if unmitigated)

   - Identify unmitigated liquidity zones between entry and TP

4. **Zone Reaction Counter** — Add to Engine 1 (Zone properties):
