## What is on the chart (legend)
HOW ENGINE 1 IS DRAWN: one ZONE = one labelled box. An Engine 1 object that overlaps a higher-ranking one, or lies within 5 pips of it (canon: levels within 0.05 are one level, levels within 0.10-0.15 are one zone), (pressure zone > 4H supply/demand > 1H supply/demand > gap in-fill > sweep zone > 4H liquidity band) is drawn as a PART of that zone - a light dotted box inside / against it with no label of its own, named after the '+' in the zone's label (operator: "a pressure zone = candle structure + level cluster JOINED ... inside it the two parts stay visibly distinct"). Count ZONES (the facts file lists each zone with its parts indented under it); a turn at a part is a turn at its zone.
Engine 1 (the operator's definition: "E1 = the sealed V1 underneath: order blocks (supply/demand) + the liquidity ladder; on top the prompt's E1: IP zones, liquidity sweep zones, canonical 4H / 1H supply/demand"). ALL of these are Engine 1 zones:
- pressure (IP) zones: red (resistance) / orange (equilibrium) / teal (support) boxes, white label `NAME | degree | ZFP | tier`;
- supply / demand candle zones: red (supply) / blue (demand) boxes, white label like `1H (Unmitigated) | MoM`;
- liquidity sweep zones: bright green boxes with a green label `LSZ supply-side | sweep to PRICE`; the dotted outline beyond the box is the sweep reach. Canon: "Liquidity Sweep Zone: level at a prior exact swing low [high] - high probability of brief breach then reversal"; "Institutions often sweep 0.05-0.10 beyond the visible extreme before reversing";
- liquidity bands of the sealed V1 (4H): thin dotted green outlines, shown only where they are a PART of a zone (a band standing alone is the sealed V1's own drawing underneath and is not drawn by this script);
- amber lines: option-strike targets (levels, not zones).
Engine 2: one grey box per day with the day's tag (`Mon SS 80%`), and the yellow line = the forecast path (a thin dotted line = the alternative chain). Canon shapes:
- SS (swing scalps): "Price oscillates within a defined range bounded by IP zones above (resistance ceiling) and below (support floor), forming a series of swing highs and swing lows that respect these boundaries. Each swing typically spans 1-3 trading sessions." Direction = range. The box runs into its two bounding zones.
- BS (breakout surge): "Price breaks decisively through an IP zone, then surges directionally toward the next IP zone."
- WL (whiplash): "A rapid, violent directional move that immediately reverses - spike-and-reject." RWL = "a Whiplash in the opposite direction of the preceding BS."
- R (recovery): "Temporary stabilization or mild retracement immediately after a BS or Whiplash."
Engine 3: position boxes - red part = stop side, green part = entry -> take-profit, the entry on their shared edge; a dotted green band on the entry side = the first-contact (front) entry: the order is met there. The RRR on a label is the RRR of the position as the canon defines it for an entry resolved on a zone: three staggered entries (front / middle / back of the entry zone, one SL, one TP), blended 33 / 33 / 34. Label `ID shape S|L RRR`. On range (SS) days every bound carries ONE order per day (a day-wide box per day; the facts file says which of them were void, skipped or filled). The repeat of a setup on a later day carries that setup's ID with the day (`DX1.Tue` = setup DX1 placed again for Tuesday, same entry / SL / TP) - it is the same setup, not a new one; a repeat exists only on days whose forecast path (yellow line) comes within 30 pips of its entry. A breakout order may REST from an earlier day than its tag (its box starts on the day it starts to rest; the tag names the day Engine 2 expects the breakout). In a week whose forecast is range on all five days the RRR floor is 2.5 (canon: 'Consolidation week (relaxed) | 2.5'), otherwise 3.0. Each generated script ends with the Engine 3 report as comments (execution calendar, every discarded candidate with its reason); the facts file repeats the discards as lines starting with `!`. A box with DASHED outlines and `if ID` in its label is a reinforcement: a conditional order that only exists once its parent setup has filled.
- discovery zones: thin dashed GREY lines with a small grey label `DISCOVERY ZONE | reason` (Engine 1, the lowest class; canon: pre-week 'levels to watch' flagged where the zone map is EMPTY - a major round number .00 / .50, a weekly-close extreme not traded since, a moving-average convergence, a structural extreme; 'NEUTRAL - reduced confidence'). The facts file lists them under DISCOVERY ZONES. A turning point at such a line is not a point 'with no zone'; give each line that price reached its own verdict like any zone.
- liquidity sweep zones are 5 pips tall, as in the operator's reference script, plus the dotted reach beyond the extreme.
- ALTERNATIVE-chain orders (Engine 3): position boxes with DASHED outlines labelled `XXA1 ... if 1H close > PRICE` (or `<`). Canon: "For every primary chain, construct at least one alternative chain that activates if the primary fails at a specific Decision Zone". Such an order is CONDITIONAL: it does not exist until a 1H candle closes beyond its Decision Zone level (the facts file says `not triggered` or `triggered <time>` before anything else). An order that never triggered is neither a loss nor a missed trade; one that triggered counts like any setup (caught / stopped). They are contingencies and are not part of the week's trade limit. The thin dotted yellow line is the alternative chain's path.
- zone labels name a zone's parts in short form after the '+': S = supply candle zone, D = demand candle zone (with its timeframe), LSZ = sweep zone, LQ = sealed-V1 4H liquidity band, IF = gap in-fill level.
- gap in-fill levels: dashed boxes labelled `GAP IN-FILL | MODERATE | ZFP` (Engine 1). Sweep zones of one side within 5 pips of each other are drawn as one box. The five day tags stand in one row above the week's forecast envelope.

## Counting rules (the project's grading rule G1, verbatim)
"One verdict per drawing with its specific delta - zone: reacted (sharp rejection OR consolidation in / around it) / pierced like paper / not reached; day: forecast shape + direction + key zone vs that day's candles; setup: filled? TP before SL?
Counting for a week: E1 = zones that reacted / (zones reached + obvious reaction points of the week that have no zone), and none bloated, bunched, floating or missing;
E2 = days right / 5; E3 = obvious trades caught / obvious trades that existed (anchor week: his own trades; other weeks: every clean zone-to-zone move visible on the 1H chart, listed BEFORE looking at my setups), every stopped-out setup named."

The clause comes from his verdict on an earlier zone map (ruling L-28, verbatim): "bloated rectangles ('40 pips with 200 candles inside'), three supports bunched and pierced" - the three supports were SEPARATE zones 1-2 pips apart that one candle crossed together. 'Floating' = zones drawn far away from where the week trades; 'missing' = an obvious turning point of the week with no Engine 1 object at it.
His reference for a map he calls about 90% accurate (DXY week 2026-04-20, his own script trident_v8.pine): five pressure zones 7 / 22 / 12 / 12 / 34 pips tall, the two lowest 3 pips apart, six sweep zones 5 pips tall, five of them lying inside or against a pressure zone, zones up to 22 pips beyond the week's high. So a zone of 20-35 pips is not 'bloated' by itself, and a sweep zone or candle zone joined to a pressure zone (a PART, see the legend - his ruling: "the zone is both joined ... inside it the two parts stay visibly distinct") is not 'bunched' with it.

Report 'bunched' in two ways so that the count can be read either way: (a) counting only the LABELLED boxes themselves (two labelled boxes 0-2 pips apart or overlapping, crossed together by one candle), (b) counting the full extents including parts.
