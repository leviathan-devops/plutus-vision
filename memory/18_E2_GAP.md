## D. Reading
1. Structure: one forecast a day with 3-4 vertices cannot exceed ~83% even with perfect prices; 90-95% needs a correct vertex about every 2 hours.
2. Anchoring: the line meets the actual price once (17:00) or twice (M-70: 02:00) a day; everything after is drift. Restarting at the actual price 4 times a day is worth +16 points (4 weeks) before any forecast.
3. Leg size: NY legs run bound to bound (40 p) where the session moves 14 p zone to zone.
4. Direction: 57-60% on the 4 weeks; no closed-session candle fact gives it; the canon's rules are conditional on events (a bound breaking, the reaction at a zone, the 10:01 release after the cage) - my build evaluates them once at the anchor instead of when the event happens.
5. Macro: breakouts are under-forecast (SS all week on 9 of 12 maps).
