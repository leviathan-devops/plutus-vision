## 2 · THE FOUR DENSITIES

### 2.1 EARTH — Liquidity & Institutional Pressure — THE FOUNDATION

- **LAWS THAT FOLLOW:** thin liquidity zones are **never** entry or SL zones (thin Earth is not

  Earth demands buffer. A thin zone sitting *inside* a 4H liquidity pool counts as **2 thin

  "S/D zones are just TP/entry points. The trade setup is anchored to liquidity."

- **IS:** supply AND demand. Where institutions deploy capital. The energy release of
  institutional order flow *is* Fire.
- **DOES:** provides **direction and force**. Without Fire, price would sit in a liquidity zone
  forever.

  continuation zones, less firepower, and they *tend to get pierced*.
- **TEMPORAL:** "95% of NY swings don't even start until the 2nd half of NY session." Fire
  activates in windows, not uniformly.
- **ENTROPY:** Fire cannot burn forever — after 2 strong piercing swing moves expect 1–2 full
  days of HTF consolidation. Fire must recharge.
- **PROBABILISTIC:** a 20–30% failure rate is *acceptable and built in*. Fire is not
  deterministic; that is why risk is engineered into the architecture rather than managed.

### 2.3 WATER — Macro Shapes — THE WAVES

## 3 · DENSITY vs ATTACK — TWO AXES THAT MUST NOT BE CONFLATED

This is the most-misread part of the architecture.

- **DENSITY** = what a thing **IS** (a mechanic: liquidity, S/D, shape, Fib).
- **ATTACK** = how a trade **ENGAGES** (the operational style).

So a **Fibonacci Rocket** is an **Air-density tool** used in a **Fire-style attack**: Air
provides the targeting laser, Fire provides the explosive impact on the S/D target. A
**Liquidity Rocket** is an **Earth-density tool** in a **Water-style attack** (flowing through
liquidity channels like water through a riverbed). An **FLR** combines **Air + Earth** tools in
an **Earth-Heavy attack** — the densest, most anchored attack possible.

The tool's element and the attack's element are different aspects of the same thing. They are
never in contradiction.

| Attack | Tool density | Attack style | Trigger | Entry/SL | Buffer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FLR** (Fibonacci Liquidity Rocket) | Air (30m Fib) + Earth (LZ) | Earth-Heavy | 30m Fib levels | Liquidity Zones | THICK: 2 thick or 3 thin zones |
| **FR** (Fibonacci Rocket) | Air (15m Fib) | Fire-Precision | 15m Fib levels | 15m Fib + LZ confluence | 2 thick or 2–3 thin zones ahead |
| **LR** (Liquidity Rocket) | Earth (LZ) | Water-Precision | price action at LZ | pure LZ | THIN: 1 thick or 2–3 thin, no entry buffer |

## 7 · MOM ZONES — THE CRITICAL LAW

> "Do not take reversal entries on MoM zones. EVER. Guaranteed loss."
> "MoM zones are ONLY to be used as LADDER STEPS for trend-aligned trades that surf existing
> momentum. This is the ONLY use case."

- **BoM = ENTRY candidates.** 100% guaranteed reaction if unmitigated.
- **MoM = LADDER STEPS** (trend-aligned) + **TP TARGETS** (for the opposing direction).

*Why:* MoM is Fire that has already partly burned — it formed DURING the move, not at the
origin, so it cannot START a new direction. Only BoM has the firepower to reverse a move.

**The exceptions (must satisfy ALL):** (a) ≥1 prior strong reaction, (b) trend-aligned,
(c) key S/R confluence, (d) opposing liquidity reaction weak/absent. **Virgin MoM = DEATH ZONE**
— automatic reject. **4H MoM is "one-and-done"**: first retest gives a *minor* reaction then a
strong pierce; not valid for swing entries, scalp at most.

**S/R confluence can promote a MoM:** if a resistance level that has held ≥2 times aligns with a
MoM supply zone (within 5 pips) and no stronger BoM exists within 15 pips — the MoM is the
valid entry. This is the explicit exception.

## 8 · THE HTF ZONE MECHANICS DECISION TREE (E1 → E2 BRIDGE)

When a **MoM zone** is tested, the *reaction* determines the next Water shape. This tree is the
literal bridge between E1 (classification) and E2 (prediction):

```
MoM zone tested (unmitigated):
  ├─ SHARP whiplash/reversal instantly
  │    → all liquidity drained at this level → zone PIERCED
  │    → next shape: BS or WL in the OPPOSITE direction
  ├─ MINOR reaction + retest, zone HOLDS
  │    → long consolidation begins (2–4 days)
  │    → next shape: SS (map swing scalps inside the range)
  ├─ Zone BREAKS but price RETURNS
  │    → short consolidation
  │    → next shape: SS → BS or SS → WL
  │    → transition setup: entry on the break of consolidation
  └─ NO pierce, no sharp whiplash
       → slow consolidation around the POI (2–4 days)
       → ends with price retesting opposing liquidity
       → then pierce through the zone until reaching BoM liquidity
       → next shape: SS → BS (breakout to the BoM target)
```

Reaction-count predicts the branch: **0 (virgin) → holds** (first test reacts, minor);
**1–2 (active) → assess surrounding liquidity**; **3+ (depleted) → pierced**.
**BoM zones are exempt from this tree — they always react**, and the resulting direction is
decided by the E3 direction-validation checks.
